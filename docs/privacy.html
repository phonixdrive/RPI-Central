Privacy Policy

Your data should serve you.

Effective September 28, 2026. This policy explains how RPI Central collects, uses, shares, retains, and deletes information.

Information stored on your device

Course selections, personal events, grades, notes, GPA overrides, tasks, dashboard settings, meal-plan state, Flex Dollars planning, study-timer preferences, and imported calendar state are primarily stored on your device. If you enable cloud backup, a backup copy is associated with your authenticated account in Firebase and can be read only by you.

Calendar access

RPI Central requests calendar permission only when you choose calendar import or sync features. Google and Outlook calendar access is mediated by accounts configured in Apple Calendar through EventKit. Imported event titles, times, and locations are used to display your combined calendar and are not used for advertising or tracking.

Account and social information

If you create an account or use Social, Firebase processes your account email address, Firebase user identifier, display name, username, friendships, requests, group membership, messages, comments, polls, activity posts, shared schedules, shared locations (only if you turn location sharing on), notification preferences, device notification tokens, and cloud backups. This information is used only to authenticate you and provide the features you request. Public social profile records do not store your authentication email address.

Sharing and service providers

Schedule sharing is optional and limited to accepted friends when enabled. Social content is shared with the people or groups you select. RPI Central uses Apple system services and Google Firebase for authentication, database storage, and push notifications. These providers are expected to protect information consistently with their applicable terms and privacy commitments. RPI Central does not sell personal information, display advertising, or track users across other companies’ apps or websites.

Location

Location sharing is off until you turn it on in Social › Map. When it is on, RPI Central stores your most recent location, its accuracy, the campus building it matches (for example, “In DCC”), and when it was updated, and makes it readable only by the accepted friends you choose. You can share for an hour, until the end of the day, or until you turn it off, and you can share only the building you are in instead of your exact position. Background updates happen only if you turn them on and grant “Always” location access; the app then updates after you move a significant distance or arrive at a part of campus. Turning on Ghost mode, signing out, or deleting your account deletes your shared location immediately, removing a friend stops sharing with them, and expired or abandoned locations are deleted automatically. Your location is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone other than the friends you choose. The shuttle map displays vehicle and route information supplied by the shuttle service and does not use your location.

Retention, choices, and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it or delete the app. Cloud data remains while your account is active or as needed to operate the service. You can revoke Calendar, Location, and notification access in iOS Settings, turn off schedule or location sharing in Social, and remove individual local or cloud entries in the app. To delete your account and associated cloud data, open Social, choose Profile, and tap Delete account. The app submits the deletion immediately and signs you out. Data that must be retained by law, if any, may be kept only for the required period.

Changes and questions

Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date. For privacy questions, use the project’s support guidance. Report security vulnerabilities privately through GitHub Security Advisories.
