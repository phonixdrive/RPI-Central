Designed for campus life

Useful between every class.

RPI Central brings planning and daily campus tools into a consistent, customizable SwiftUI experience.

01

Term-aware calendar

Official academic dates, class schedules, personal events, external calendars, and persistent day/week/month layouts.

02

Course planning

Search bundled catalogs, inspect sections and prerequisites, and keep current-term room and instructor details refreshed.

03

Adaptive Home

Reorder persistent dashboard cards and choose compact, wide, or large layouts for the information you use most.

04

Deadlines and reminders

See what is next, connect work to classes, and schedule local notifications without blocking the interface while data loads.

05

Campus utilities

Check shuttle positions, dining hours, meal swipes, Flex Dollars pacing, and a focused study timer.

06

Friends and classes

Direct messages, groups, class communities, friend schedules, and privacy-controlled current calendar activity.

One schedule

Phone, web, and external calendars.

Import authorized Google and Outlook calendars through EventKit, maintain local-first app data, and create weekly recovery backups when signed in.

Your control

Privacy before presence.

Schedule sharing is opt-in and friend-gated. Live person-location sharing is not implemented, and private credentials stay outside the repository.
